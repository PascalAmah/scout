01 — Logo

Wordmark & icon

The wordmark pairs Fraunces, an editorial serif, with the monocular mark in place of the O — an emerald ring with a dark lens, tilted slightly upward to suggest looking outward. The standalone icon carries the mark on its own for favicons, app tiles, and social profiles.

Primary — on white

Reversed — on charcoal

Icon variants — charcoal, emerald, outlined, mark-only (favicon)

02 — Color

Palette

Emerald is the accent — used sparingly, for the mark and key actions. Charcoal carries text and structure. White does the rest of the work.

Emerald

#18A058 · RGB 24,160,88

Charcoal

#1F2937 · RGB 31,41,55

White

#FFFFFF · RGB 255,255,255

03 — Typography

Type system

Fraunces is reserved for the logotype and large editorial moments. Inter runs the product — UI, body copy, data.

Display — Fraunces, used only in the logotype and hero headlines

Discover your next role

UI & body — Inter

Every saved startup becomes structured knowledge.

Inter runs everything else: navigation, buttons, form labels, tables, and body copy across the dashboard and extension. It's neutral and highly legible at small sizes, which is what a data-dense workspace needs.

04 — Clearspace

Minimum space & size

Keep clearspace equal to the height of the monocular ring on all sides. Don't let UI chrome, text, or other logos enter this zone.

Minimum digital width: 96px. Minimum print width: 25mm.

05 — Usage

Do & don't

DO

Use the icon at full contrast, on approved backgrounds, at consistent proportions.

DON'T

Don't recolor, stretch, skew, or place the mark on clashing backgrounds.
