Opportunity matching

A fit score you can actually question

Most matching tools give you a number and ask you to trust it. Scout shows its work — every score ships with the skills that matched, the gaps that didn't, and a plain-language reason, because an overconfident score can talk you out of a good application just as easily as into a bad one.

  • Two-stage scoring: fast embedding similarity narrows the field, then a closer pass checks explicit requirements.
  • Gaps are always shown alongside matches — a score with no gaps is treated as a bug, not a perfect fit.
  • Recalculates automatically when you update your CV or save a new role.

Does Scout apply to jobs for me?+

No. Scout prepares your resume, cover letter, and outreach — you review and submit every application yourself, outside Scout. There's no auto-submit.

Will generated content invent experience I don't have?+

No. Every generation call is grounded in your base CV; the prompt is explicitly instructed not to add claims, titles, or metrics that aren't already there.

Is my CV used to train other models?+

No. Your CV and portfolio data is encrypted at rest and never used to train external models. You can delete it at any time.

Which sources can I save from today?+

YC company pages and generic careers pages at launch, via the browser extension. Wellfound, Techstars, and Product Hunt are on the roadmap as scheduled sync sources.

What happens to my base resume when Scout generates a tailored version?+

Nothing — it's untouched. Every generated version is a separate, versioned copy with a diff view against your base resume.
